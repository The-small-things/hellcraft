# Hellcraft: the music and sounds it needs

Everything in Hellcraft that makes a sound uses one of Minecraft's own sounds for now: ghast screams for the damned, a
didgeridoo note for a red danger mark, music discs for Lucifer. This is the list of what would replace them, most needed
first. Send whatever you have or can make: one sound or a whole set, and I'll wire it in.

## How to send them

- **Format:** `.ogg` (Ogg Vorbis). If you only have `.wav` or `.mp3`, send that and I'll convert it.
- **Sound effects:** mono. Minecraft only places mono sounds in the world: a stereo sound plays at the same volume
  everywhere, so you can't tell where a scream came from. Peaks at about -1 dBFS, no silence at the start.
- **Music:** stereo, 44.1 or 48 kHz, all tracks at about the same loudness (around -14 LUFS). Minecraft can't loop a
  track seamlessly; it plays a track to the end and starts again, so an ending that leads back into the start works best.
- **Variations:** for anything heard often (a scream, a footstep, a hit), 3 to 6 slightly different takes. Minecraft picks
  one at random each time, so it doesn't sound like a loop.
- **Rights:** only sounds you made, paid for, or that are free to use (CC0, or CC-BY with the creator's name, which I'll
  put in the credits). Not ripped from other games or films.
- **Names:** anything clear, e.g. `damned_scream_3.ogg`, `lucifer_true_form.ogg`.

## 1. Must have

### Lucifer's fight (3 music tracks)
These are already wired in: drop them in and they play.

| Track | When it plays | Feel | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Duel** | The Fallen Seraph comes down and duels you | Beautiful and tragic: an angel's music gone wrong. Choir, strings, a slow pulse | 3–5 min |
| **Enraged** | At half health he snaps and the traitors rise from the ice | The same theme turned violent: faster, drums, brass, dissonance | 2–4 min |
| **The Emperor** | The mountain opens its eyes: the Three-Faced Emperor in the ice | Huge, slow and cold: low organ and brass, deep choir, wind. Dread more than action | 4–6 min |

### The damned (sound effects)
The souls suffering in every circle.

| Sound | Takes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Scream of the damned | 6–8 | Human, anguished, from a distance. Not a monster's roar. Different voices if possible |
| Sigh of Limbo | 4 | Soft, longing, no pain: the virtuous pagans, who only sigh |
| Wail or sob | 4 | Lower and longer than a scream, for the souls between screams |

### The danger language (sound effects)
Each boss's blows are warned by a coloured mark on the ground. Each colour needs its own sound, so players learn them by
ear.

| Sound | Takes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Red mark: "get out" | 1–2 | A low horn or toll |
| White mark: "block now" | 1–2 | A sharp, bright ring: a blade drawn, a bell struck |
| Purple mark: "can't be blocked, jump" | 1–2 | An eerie rising chime |
| Red blow lands | 3 | Fire bursting out of the ground |
| White blow lands | 3 | A crack of light, a heavy clang |
| Purple blow lands | 3 | A deep, tearing whoosh |
